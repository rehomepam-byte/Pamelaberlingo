ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Pamela Berlingo

Pamela Berlingo writes stories inspired by community, history, landscape, and the mysteries that can remain hidden in familiar places.

Her Tunkhannock Chronicles follow Kate Mercer, Brian Magnus, Carl Davis, and Andrew as research and friendship lead them from the library into the history surrounding Tunkhannock, Pennsylvania.
